The dungeon is your taskbar

Margin March is a Windows desktop idle RPG built in Unreal Engine 5. Your hero and pet march along a slim 88px strip that sits directly over the OS taskbar, or down the left or right edge in a top-down corridor view. Along the way it fights monsters and bosses, solves small puzzles and takes ladders and holes between levels. Work, browse, game. They keep adventuring in the margin.

Zero-intrusion by design

Click-throughA transparent window that never steals a click from the apps underneath.
Auto-hidesSteps aside when a fullscreen app or game is running.
Snooze & quiet hoursSilence the march for a while, or on a daily schedule.
Global hotkeyShow, hide or snooze from anywhere, with a shortcut you choose.
Under 2% CPULight enough to leave running all day.
No accountsNo sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry.

Features

01

Passive progression

Idle loot drops while you work. Step away and get an offline “welcome back” reward summary when you return. Earn level-up badges, chase collection goals that grant permanent bonuses, and look back through your journal.

02

Diverse biomes

March through five worlds, each with its own look, enemies and hazards.

DungeonIce TundraVolcanic InfernoCyber OS GlitchAbyssal Ruins
03

Extensive customization

Equip cosmetic hats, weapon trails and companion pets. Collect spellbooks and relics, and tune the march with settings for size, position, orientation, opacity, monitor, pace and frame rate. Start with Windows is optional.

04

Emergent AI

Your hero isn't on rails. It backtracks to solve gate puzzles, stops at anvils to re-armor, and dodges boss attacks on its own.
