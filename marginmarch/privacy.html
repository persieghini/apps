Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

Margin March has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertisements, no analytics and no telemetry. We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not collect any.

Zero data collection

Margin March is a Windows desktop idle game. It does not require an account and never asks you to sign in. It contains no advertising and no analytics or telemetry, and it sends no data about you or your PC to us. We do not operate servers for the game.

Local save files

Your progress and settings are stored only on your own PC, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MarginMarch\Saved\. Nothing in that folder is uploaded by the game. If you uninstall the game and delete that folder, all of your data is permanently removed.

Windows startup registry entry

If you turn on “Start with Windows”, the game adds a Run key entry to the registry of the current Windows user only. Turning the setting off removes that entry. If you never enable it, no entry is created.

Optional global hotkeys

If you configure global hotkeys, the game listens locally for those specific shortcuts so it can show, hide or snooze from any app. No keystrokes are recorded, stored or transmitted.

Fullscreen auto-hide

To step aside when a fullscreen app is running, the game checks locally whether a window covers the screen, using screen bounding boxes. It never reads or captures window titles, URLs or screen content.

Discord Rich Presence (optional)

If enabled, the game sends short status text, for example “Fighting Magma Golem (Act 3)”, only to the Discord app running on your PC, through its local IPC socket. What Discord does with it is governed by Discord's Privacy Policy. You can turn this off in Settings.

Twitch chat integration (optional)

If enabled, the game connects anonymously and read-only to public Twitch IRC chat to count vote commands. It does not log in, does not post, and does not log or store chat messages or usernames. Twitch's own handling of that connection is governed by Twitch's Privacy Notice.

Steam integration

Purchases, Steam Cloud saves and achievements are handled by Valve through Steam, under Steam's Privacy Policy. We do not receive that data.

Children's privacy

Margin March is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Since the game collects nothing, changes are unlikely.
