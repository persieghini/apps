Questions

Good to know

Which animals can it recognise?

Common animals: pets, farm animals, garden birds and zoo favourites like lions, zebras and giraffes. For anything else, search the built-in catalog of nearly 3,000 species, or type your own name for it.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Logging, recognition, your journal and your stats all work offline. The map shows areas you've viewed or saved before; weather lookups and new map areas need a connection.

Where are my photos stored?

In the app's private storage on your device. Shots you take in the app are also saved to your Photos library. If you log from Immich, the original stays on your server and the app keeps a small thumbnail.

How do I move my journal to a new phone?

Go to Settings, then Backup & restore. Export everything into one encrypted file, then restore that file on the new phone.

Is there an Android version?

It's on the way to Google Play. The iPhone and iPad version comes first.
