5. Suggestions can be wrong

FaunaFolio suggests species, breeds, rarity, place names, weather and similar details automatically. Species recognition runs on your device, so it has limits: it can be wrong, vague or confident about the wrong animal, especially with blurry, distant, partial or unusual photos.

Please don't rely on FaunaFolio to decide whether an animal is dangerous, venomous, protected or edible, whether a plant or animal is safe to touch or eat, how to treat a sick or injured animal, or what to do about a pet's health. For anything that matters, ask a qualified person: a guide, a ranger, a vet or a doctor.

"Verified" and "Imported" labels describe where a sighting's location came from (your phone's GPS or the photo's own data), not that the species is confirmed. Dates and places marked "Date unknown" or "trip location" are exactly that, and a photo's date and location can be missing or wrong. Rarity, XP, streaks and achievements are just for fun.

6. Treat wildlife with care

FaunaFolio is about watching animals, not disturbing them. You are responsible for how you behave when you use it.

  • Keep a safe, respectful distance. Don't chase, corner, feed, touch, call, flash or handle wild animals, and stay away from nests, dens and young.
  • Follow the laws, permits and rules of the places you visit: protected areas, private land, closures, drone and photography rules, and guide instructions.
  • Think before sharing exact locations of rare, endangered, nesting or otherwise sensitive animals. FaunaFolio lets you hide precise coordinates when you export or share. Poachers and crowds can harm what you love.
  • Never put yourself or others at risk to add an entry.
