Helpdesk.exe — Night Shift Edition

Night shift at the villain support line

The phone is ringing. Bosses, lich lords and overworked minions all have problems with the heroes invading their dungeons, and you are the one on the line. Open a ticket, check the Rulebook, fix the problem before the SLA runs out, and keep Corporate Trust from hitting zero.

Ticket queueCalls pile up. Pick them up, juggle them and close them before time runs out.
The RulebookTraps, bosses, comebacks, minions, potions, levers, chests and spell dials, all in one in-game manual that keeps getting new editions.
Practice DeskNo timers, nothing to lose. Learn each puzzle at your own pace.
Career & upgradesEarn gems, climb from Junior Technician and buy upgrades that come with trade-offs.

Ways to play

01

2-player online public co-op

Matchmake with players worldwide and triage dungeon tickets together. Join someone who is looking, or wait in the Break Room for the next person.

02

Co-op with a friend

Host your own Break Room and invite a friend for a private shift. Set the lobby to friends only and choose the role you host as.

03

Solo single player

Run the whole helpdesk on your own. Phones, rulebook, coffee and router resets are all yours to juggle.

04

In-game voice chat

Coordinate strategy and survive frantic tickets in real time. Mics, roles and practice all happen in the Break Room before you ready up.

Player safety

■

Block and mute

Built-in block and mute tools keep a shift smooth and toxic-free. If someone ruins your night, silence them or block them for good.

■

No accounts, no tracking

No sign-in, no ads, no telemetry. Voice is never recorded or stored. Read the privacy policy and the online code of conduct.
