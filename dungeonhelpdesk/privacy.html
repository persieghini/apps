Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

Dungeon Helpdesk has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising trackers and no telemetry. We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not collect any. Voice chat is never recorded, stored or logged.

Zero data collection

Dungeon Helpdesk is a Windows desktop game. It does not require an account and never asks you to sign in. It contains no advertising trackers and no telemetry, and it sends no data about you or your PC to us. We do not sell or share personal data.

Local save files

Your progress and settings are stored only on your own PC, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\DungeonHelpdesk\. If you uninstall the game and delete that folder, all of your data is permanently removed.

Multiplayer and voice chat

Online co-op matchmaking and in-game voice chat use secure, encrypted connections that exist only to make multiplayer work. Matchmaking and Break Room lobbies run through Steam. When you play online, your game actions and voice are sent in real time to the other player in your session.

Voice data is transmitted live and is never recorded, stored or logged on any server. Blocking and muting are handled inside the game.

Steam

Purchases, Steam Cloud saves, achievements and online lobbies are handled by Valve through Steam, under Steam's Privacy Policy. We do not receive that data.

Children's privacy

Dungeon Helpdesk is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Online play involves live voice chat with other players, so we recommend parents supervise younger players.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Since the game collects nothing, changes are unlikely.
